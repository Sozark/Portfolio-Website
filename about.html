About

// IDENTITY

Noah C. Jones

UI/UX Designer & Front-End Developer · Accessibility-Focused

// BIO.txt

I work across the full process, from UX research and Figma systems through to production-deployed code, rather than handing off between design and engineering. Every project in this portfolio went through that full arc.

I have a B.S. in Computer Science (LSU) with a minor in Digital Media Arts & Engineering, and treat WCAG/ADA compliance as a design standard rather than a checklist. BandBuilder, an accessibility-first music game for children with autism, was usability tested with real users ages 5–8 and built with zero failure states by design. As an Independent Design Engineer I run client projects end-to-end: UX research, AI-assisted design workflows, Figma systems, and production front-end code — alongside shipped work like StayFinder AI, EternalNJEM, and Bubble Hero, 1st place at Global Game Jam.

I'm looking for Design Engineer, UX Engineer, or Product Designer roles where design and implementation are handled by the same person, and I care about building digital products that work equally well for everyone using them.
